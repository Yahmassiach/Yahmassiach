## I find the most expensive broken thing and ship the fix.

Los Angeles. I came to building from the business side — I learned to ship because the thing I
needed didn't exist and waiting for someone else to build it was costing me more than learning to
do it myself.

**In 85 days, working alone, I shipped ~115,000 lines across 9 repositories, authored 47
specialized agents that do real work, and filed 14 provisional patent applications.**

---

### The loop I run

**1 · Find the pain.** Read the tickets, the inbox, the handoffs, the spreadsheet everyone
complains about. Not the org chart — the actual friction.

**2 · Price it.** Put a dollar-per-month figure on each broken thing, with the arithmetic shown
and every input labelled *measured*, *their estimate*, or *my assumption*. The number is what makes
the room go quiet.

**3 · Build the fix.** Not a recommendation. Not a deck. A running thing, on their real data.

**4 · Drive it to shipped.** Check in every day until it holds.

I don't hand over a slide and leave. The deliverable is software that works on Monday.

### Selected work

**Ramble** — a longitudinal pattern-recognition engine for spoken reflection. ~95,000 lines,
545 files, 193 tests. Backend on a live API, deployed, evaluated against a purpose-built
8-persona synthetic corpus rather than vibes.

**Prox** — consent-first event platform. Live, with real phone-OTP accounts on Neon + Prisma.

**IKA** — a terminal-native agent workforce: 47 authored agents across patent drafting, company
formation, design, venture analysis, and a seven-seat decision council that produces scored
verdicts and permanent, auditable decision records.

**inbox-agent** — an operational triage tool packaged across six business verticals, built
end to end in one sitting.
